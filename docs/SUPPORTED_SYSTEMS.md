Two entries read *SENEC.Home V3 hybrid* because the appliance uses that name for two
different system identifiers.

Not finding your system here does not mean it will not work — the list only covers models
known by name. Any system reachable on your network, or registered to a mein-senec.de
account, is worth trying. Feedback on further systems is always welcome.
